Symptoms You Shouldn't Ignore

Persistent or recurrent symptoms deserve a professional evaluation.

🩸 Blood during bowel movementsFresh blood or recurrent bleeding should be assessed.
⚡ Pain during or after passing stoolEspecially sharp, severe or recurring pain.
◉ Lump or swelling near the anusMay occur with piles, abscess or other conditions.
💧 Discharge or pusPersistent discharge can occur with fistula or infection.
〰️ Persistent itching or irritationSeveral anorectal conditions can cause irritation.
🌡️ Fever with severe anal painSeek prompt medical attention if these occur together.
